I'm Raghad, an analog and digital photographer by dusk and dawn and an engineer at all other times. I love to document the world as I see it, especially in slow-form, analog processes. I love to spend time with an image, to become familiar with the landscape, to pore over it in the dark room. My work spans 35 mm, medium, and large format B&W film, aerial drone photography, and digital documentary. Let's talk! I'm always looking for new inspiration, friends, and stories.
